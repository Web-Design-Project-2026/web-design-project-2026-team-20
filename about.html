About Us

Fika Hörnet is a cozy cafe located in the heart of Jönköping. Founded with a passion for Swedish fika culture, we offer a warm and inviting space where customers can relax and enjoy freshly baked pastries and quality coffee. Our goal is to create a welcoming environment where people can take a break from their day, meet friends, and enjoy delicious treats.

Our Story

Established in 2015, Fika Hörnet was born out of a love for the Swedish tradition of fika — a cherished moment to pause and enjoy a coffee break with friends or family. At Fika Hörnet, we bake our pastries fresh every morning, using traditional Swedish recipes passed down through generations. Our coffee is carefully selected and brewed to savor, a case of perfection.
